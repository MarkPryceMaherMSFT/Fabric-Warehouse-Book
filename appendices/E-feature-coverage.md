## Reading an unresolved entry

"Not independently verified" does not mean the announcement is false. It means the reviewed public references did not establish enough detail for a prescriptive, supported walkthrough at this cutoff. The useful response is to teach the purpose, identify the actual missing contract, and avoid fabricating syntax or guarantees.

For the underlying references and contradictions, use [Appendix C](C-feature-status.md), the [November source refresh](D-bibliography.md#revision-2026-11-09) and the retained [October extension](D-bibliography.md#revision-2026-10-09).
