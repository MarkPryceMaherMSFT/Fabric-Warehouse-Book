## Corrections that matter more than matching a title

The March 2025 endpoint comparison's shared-engine insight is retained, but its case-sensitive-only and unlimited/width wording is not treated as a current contract. The time-zone article motivates separate context; ordinary Delta timestamps are not claimed to preserve an original zone name. The older MLV preview label is not applied to every 2026 component. History writing is not granted to a read-only endpoint.

Publishing timing observations remain experiments, not SLAs. Count equality is strengthened to marker-based bounded checking. Health advice remains conditional and owner-specific. A convenient time-travel clamp is distinguished from answering the caller's original timestamp. Sources, prototypes, supported contracts and actual tenant availability retain their different evidential roles.
