There are **21 distinct topic groups** below. Item-definition CRUD APIs appear twice in the article and are counted once. Language/editor bundles contain several named components; the rows do not imply 21 independent releases. The customer account and benchmark figures are also retained with their attribution and limits.

The June labels are historical. The final column connects them to later reviewed evidence and the applicable contract, not universal tenant availability. The separate [roadmap snapshot remains dated 9 November 2026](H-roadmap-snapshot.md#snapshot-9-november-2026); this article review does not refresh or overwrite its 25 observations.

## How to use the chronology

The roundup intentionally combines released, preview and future capabilities. Its closing rollout language is not permission to promote every Looking ahead item to GA six months later. Conversely, later specific contracts can close an old gap: current Warehouse definition APIs and pre/post scripts should be taught through those contracts rather than left as June promises.

The follow-up adds original explanations and book-specific examples to Chapters 4, 7, 9-11, 19, 28, 34 and 37. Existing correct coverage of retention, history, sync, bitmap filtering and lifecycle remains in place. No chapter is renumbered, no source-code pin is moved, and no article screenshot or chart is copied.

This is a public-source comparison, not a tenant rollout test, migration execution or performance reproduction. Consult the specific product reference and verify eligibility before adopting an optional feature.
