# Part VI - Operating the platform

[Book contents](../../index.md) | [Cover](../../README.md)

**Chapters 29-36**

Operate the solution as a service. Bring monitoring and incident investigation together with consumption, capacity, workload isolation, acceleration, recovery and repeatable deployment.

## Before you begin

Use Parts IV and V to interpret execution evidence. Check the permissions and side effects of every diagnostic or configuration action; a successful lab is not permission to change shared production resources.
