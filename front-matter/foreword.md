# Foreword

[Book contents](../index.md) | [Previous: Publication notes](publication-notes.md) | [Next: Preface](preface.md)

For more than 20 years, I have worked with data warehouses in many different forms. My early experience included SQL Server, Informix and even solutions built around ISAM files. Since then, my work has spanned purpose-built systems and cloud platforms, including SQL Server FastTrack, Parallel Data Warehouse (PDW), Oracle, Netezza, Teradata and Snowflake. Today, that journey continues with Microsoft Fabric Warehouse.

The technology has changed considerably, but the need to understand our data platforms has not. We still need to make sound decisions about how data is modeled, loaded, secured, queried and operated.

Fabric Warehouse is a software-as-a-service platform, which removes much of the infrastructure administration associated with traditional data warehouses. It does not remove the need to understand how the platform works, where its responsibilities end, or how to build reliable analytical solutions on top of it.

That is why I began putting this book together: to give people a practical head start with Fabric Warehouse and its SQL analytics endpoints. My aim is to connect the first steps with the deeper concepts that matter as a solution grows, from architecture and data modeling to performance, security, operations and migration.

No single book can cover every feature, question or scenario, particularly on a platform that continues to evolve. What I hope this book provides is a clear foundation, useful explanations and the confidence to explore further, ask better questions and make informed decisions.

Enjoy the book, and good luck with your Fabric Warehouse journey.

**Mark Pryce-Maher**
