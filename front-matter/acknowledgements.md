# Acknowledgements

[Book contents](../index.md) | [Previous: Preface](preface.md) | [Next: How to use this book](how-to-use-this-book.md)

This manuscript benefits from the people who make analytical systems easier to understand by publishing documentation, research, explanations and practical code. Their public work gives readers a way to follow an argument back to evidence rather than accepting it on trust.

## Documentation, research and technical explanations

Thanks are due to the Microsoft Learn documentation contributors and the researchers behind Polaris, its transaction extensions, unified query optimization and CoddSpeed. Their publications help connect the SQL experience to the engineering ideas beneath it. The [bibliography](../appendices/D-bibliography.md) identifies the papers, credited authors and scope of review rather than treating historical research as a complete description of the current service.

The book also draws on attributed public explanations by Jovan Popovic, Srdjan Matin, Bogdan Crivat, Tino Tereshko, Rakesh Krishnan and other Fabric contributors recorded in the bibliography. Where a publication supplies an account name rather than a verified full name, that attribution is preserved rather than expanded by guesswork.

## Practical code and reproducible learning

The Microsoft Fabric CAT team and Fabric Toolbox contributors have made public implementation patterns available for inspection and adaptation under their stated terms. Mark Pryce-Maher's public gists and repositories provide additional examples and operational starting points.

The [script source register](../appendices/B-script-source-register.md) records what was used, how it was adapted and which limitations remain. The [third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) preserve the distinction between acknowledgement, permission and a downstream licence.

## Editorial acknowledgement

These credits recognise public contributions cited by the manuscript. They do not claim that any named person reviewed this book, supplied private advice, approved its conclusions or endorsed its publication.

Personal thanks, manuscript-review credits and any guest foreword will be added by the author once the names and contributions have been confirmed. This draft deliberately does not invent them.
